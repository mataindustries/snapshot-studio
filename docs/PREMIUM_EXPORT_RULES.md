# Premium Export Rules

- Page 1 must be screenshot/share-worthy.
- The archetype image must be large, clear, and uncropped.
- Do not duplicate the hero image on the first page.
- Avoid excessive dead space.
- Do not put dense report blocks on the cover.
- Do not use thick black borders.
- Keep the outreach PDF short.
- Use soft sections, cards, gold dividers, ivory backgrounds, and premium spacing.
- The PDF should feel like a custom mini-diagnosis a business owner would want to open.
