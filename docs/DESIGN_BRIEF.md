# Design Brief

## Brand Direction

Business Archetype should feel premium, editorial, and diagnostic. It is a luxury mini-read on a business website, not a generic audit, worksheet, or software report.

Client-facing copy uses **Reputation Magnet**. The current illustration belongs to the legacy **Trust Magnet** artwork family; its embedded labels will be refreshed in a future visual-asset pass. This internal asset note does not belong in client deliverables.

## Visual Palette

Use ivory, cream, deep navy, muted gold, and warm neutrals. The overall feel should be refined and warm, with enough contrast for clear reading.

## Typography Direction

Use a strong editorial hierarchy: refined headings, confident score treatment, and readable body text. Headings can feel magazine-like; supporting copy should stay concise and practical.

## Layout Principles

Use generous spacing, soft section boundaries, and calm hierarchy. Avoid cramped columns, hard black borders, dense report blocks on first view, and anything that feels like a wireframe.

## Image Direction

Archetype images should feel like hero assets, not thumbnails. Show the full artwork clearly, with warm ivory or cream behind it, and never crop off important title or character details.
